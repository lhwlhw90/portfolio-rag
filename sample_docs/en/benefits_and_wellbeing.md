# Acme Robotics Benefits and Wellbeing

## Pension
Acme contributes 5% of the gross salary to the company pension plan. Employees can add up to 4% of their own
salary, and Acme matches employee contributions up to an additional 2%. Contributions vest after one year.

## Health
All employees are covered by the group health insurance from their first day. Dental cover is included for
employees and their children. An annual health check-up at a partner clinic is free of charge.

## Fitness and mobility
Employees receive a fitness subsidy of 30 EUR per month for gym or sports club memberships. Acme offers bike
leasing through a partner; the leasing rate is deducted from the gross salary. The public transport ticket
is subsidised with 49 EUR per month.

## Family
Employees with children under six receive a childcare allowance of 150 EUR per month. In addition to
parental leave, parents can take up to ten paid days per year to care for a sick child.

## Employee assistance
The employee assistance program offers free and confidential counselling by phone, 24 hours a day. Up to six
counselling sessions per year are covered.

## Anniversaries and referrals
Employees receive one extra vacation day after five years of service. A successful employee referral is
rewarded with a bonus of 2,000 EUR, paid after the new hire completes probation.
