# RoboArm Customer Support Policy

## Support plans
Every RoboArm X2 includes the Standard support plan for the warranty period. Standard support is available
by e-mail and the customer portal on working days from 8:00 to 17:00 CET.
The Premium support plan adds 24/7 phone support and a guaranteed response time of two hours for production
outages. Premium support costs 1,800 EUR per robot per year.

## Response times
Standard support answers tickets within one business day. Premium support answers production-outage tickets
within two hours and all other tickets within four business hours.

## Returns and repairs (RMA)
Defective units are returned through the RMA process. Customers request an RMA number in the customer portal and
ship the unit in its original packaging. Repairs under warranty are completed within ten business days after the
unit arrives at the service center in Leipzig.

## Spare parts
Spare parts are guaranteed to be available for at least seven years after the end of production.
Standard spare parts ship within 48 hours from the central warehouse.

## Training
Acme offers a two-day operator training and a three-day programming training. Up to two operator training seats
are included with every new RoboArm X2.
