# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees may choose to start between 7:00 and 10:00.
Remote work is allowed up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from the VP of People.

## Vacation and leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year. Unused vacation days can be carried over
to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for all parents.
Sick leave does not require a doctor's note for absences shorter than three days.
Vacation requests longer than two weeks must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

## Equipment
Every new employee receives a laptop and a 27-inch monitor. Equipment requests are handled by the IT
helpdesk and are usually fulfilled within two business days. Employees working from home can claim a
one-time home office allowance of 500 EUR for a chair, desk or headset.

## Travel and expenses
Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights
shorter than six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. The daily meal allowance during
business trips is 45 EUR. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days, together with receipts.

## Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 EUR that can be spent on courses, books and
conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Employees may also spend up to four working
days per year on training.

## Performance reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in March and September. Salary adjustments are decided once a
year after the March review. New employees have a probation period of six months.

## Code of conduct
Gifts from suppliers worth more than 50 EUR must be declared to the compliance team. Harassment of any kind
is not tolerated and can be reported anonymously through the ethics hotline.
