Acme RoboArm X2 - Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum payload of the RoboArm X2?
The RoboArm X2 can lift a maximum payload of 7.5 kg at full reach of 900 mm.

How heavy is the arm itself?
The RoboArm X2 weighs 21 kg without the controller. The controller box weighs an additional 6 kg.

How long is the warranty?
The RoboArm X2 comes with a 3-year limited warranty covering parts and labour. Batteries are covered for 12 months.

Which programming languages are supported?
The X2 SDK supports Python and C++. A ROS 2 driver is available as an open-source package.

How do I reset the controller?
Hold the blue reset button on the controller for 10 seconds until the status LED blinks orange.

What power supply does the X2 need?
The controller requires a 100-240 V AC single-phase supply and draws at most 350 W during operation.

Is the arm safe to use next to people?
Yes. The X2 is a collaborative robot certified to ISO 10218. It stops automatically when a collision force
above 150 N is detected, and an emergency stop button is included with every unit.

Can the X2 be used outdoors or in wet environments?
The arm has an IP54 rating. It is protected against dust and water splashes but must not be submerged or
cleaned with a pressure washer.

How are firmware updates installed?
Firmware updates are installed from the web dashboard. Download the update package, upload it under
Settings > Firmware, and keep the controller powered on until the update finishes (about 8 minutes).

How accurate is the arm?
The repeatability of the X2 is plus or minus 0.03 mm.
