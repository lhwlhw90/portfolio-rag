Acme Robotics Office Guide (Berlin headquarters)

Address and access
The headquarters is located at Torstrasse 101, 10119 Berlin. The building is open from 6:00 to 22:00 on
working days. Employees enter with their badge; access on weekends must be requested from facility management.

Visitors
Visitors must be registered in the visitor system at least one day in advance. Reception issues a visitor badge,
which must be returned when leaving the building.

Meeting rooms
Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar system. Bookings longer than four hours require approval from
facility management. Rooms that are not checked in within 15 minutes are released automatically.

Cafeteria
The cafeteria on the ground floor serves lunch from 11:30 to 14:00. Employees pay 4.50 EUR for a main dish; the
rest is subsidised by the company. A vegetarian option is offered every day.

Parking
The underground garage has 40 parking spaces, including 8 spaces with electric vehicle chargers. Parking spaces
are assigned by facility management; there is a waiting list.

Safety
A fire drill takes place twice a year. The assembly point is the courtyard in front of the main entrance.
First aid kits are located next to the elevators on every floor.

Quiet rooms
Two quiet rooms on the third floor can be used for focused work or short breaks. Phone calls are not allowed in
the quiet rooms.
